NoxHere for Mac keeps your team's GitHub activity close at hand. Stay on top of every PR your team ships without opening a browser.

WHAT YOU GET
• Live feed of every PR opened, merged, and released — narrated in first-person by AI so scanning is fast
• Notifications for review requests, assigned issues, and activity on your pull requests
• Current tab shows exactly who has open PRs, review requests, and assigned issues right now — with counts at a glance
• Click any card to open the PR's full activity timeline, with a one-click link out to GitHub
• Multi-account and multi-org support — sign in with more than one GitHub identity, switch instantly
• Global hotkey (⌘⇧X) to open the popover from anywhere

HOW IT WORKS
Sign in with GitHub and pick an organization. NoxHere reads that org's pull-request activity through NoxHere and renders it in your menubar — no separate account or dashboard to set up.

PRIVACY
NoxHere stores only short-lived NoxHere credentials in the macOS Keychain, gated by the app sandbox. GitHub access and activity data are brokered through NoxHere. NoxHere records limited first-party account, organization, registration, and last-active metadata to operate the service and understand product adoption. There are no third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

REQUIRES
A GitHub account that's a member of at least one organization, with NoxHere installed on that org. No separate account or signup needed — just GitHub.
