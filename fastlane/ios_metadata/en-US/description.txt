NoxHere for iPhone and iPad keeps your team's GitHub activity close at hand. Check what changed, see what needs your attention, and jump to the right GitHub page wherever you are.

YOUR TEAM FEED
Follow pull requests as they are opened, reviewed, merged, and released. Switch between posts, opened work, and release notes without digging through notifications.

YOUR CURRENT WORK
See your open pull requests and assigned issues together, with repository names and update times at a glance.

BUILT FOR NOXCONNECT
Sign in with GitHub and select an organization connected to NoxConnect. NoxConnect handles GitHub access and provides NoxHere with the activity your organization has chosen to share. There is no separate NoxHere account to manage.

IPHONE, IPAD, AND MAC
Use NoxHere on mobile while away from your desk and the menu bar app while working on your Mac. Both connect to the same organizations and tracked repositories through NoxConnect.

PRIVACY
NoxHere contains no advertising or third-party analytics trackers. Authentication and GitHub access are handled by NoxConnect. Limited first-party account, organization, registration, and last-active information is used to operate the service and understand product adoption.

REQUIRES
A GitHub account belonging to at least one organization. NoxConnect must be installed for an organization before its activity can appear. A read-only demo is available without signing in.
